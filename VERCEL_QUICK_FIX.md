## ⚠️ Why Your Custom Domain Failed:

You tried to use: `combridge-polytechnic-vercel.app`

**This won't work because:**
- `.vercel.app` is Vercel's domain
- You can't create custom `.vercel.app` subdomains
- Only Vercel can assign `.vercel.app` URLs

**Valid formats:**
- ✅ `your-project-name.vercel.app` (auto-assigned by Vercel)
- ✅ `your-own-domain.com` (must buy from domain registrar)
- ❌ `custom-name.vercel.app` (not allowed)
